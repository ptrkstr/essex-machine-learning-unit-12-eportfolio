# Team Project: Airbnb Price Analysis

*Units 1 to 6.* Five of us (Zee, John-Jon, Julio, Dzemal and I) analysed the
2019 New York City Airbnb dataset for an executive audience, using regression
and clustering. The final question asked how a valuation model could predict
asking prices from listing, host, review and location features, and which areas
showed the greatest pricing uncertainty. The report received 74 per cent.

- [Team code repository](https://github.com/julesrif/ML-Airbnb-Business-Analysis)
- [Meeting notes I wrote](meeting-notes.md)
- [Tutor feedback](artefacts/unit-06-tutor-feedback.pdf)

## How the team worked

We agreed a team contract, used Telegram for daily contact, a shared GitHub task
board for every task, and held weekly online meetings with a decision log.
Pull requests needed one reviewer before merging.

## My contribution

The task board records who did what. My role was coordination and writing;
Julio and Dzemal led the modelling.

- I scheduled and hosted the meetings and wrote the notes for five
  of the six, turning each discussion into actions and decisions.
- I opened 13 of the board's tasks and kept it current through
  to submission.
- I wrote the introduction and the conclusion, and revised them
  after a teammate's review.
- My Meeting 05 notes recorded the mismatches between the draft
  and the final notebook output that the team then corrected before submission.
- I reviewed pull requests and drafts, and chased the final
  originality check.

## Feedback

The tutor praised the clear business question, the structured method and the
practical recommendations, and asked for deeper critical evaluation of why the
models performed as they did. I took that forward into the
[final project](final-project.md). Each of us also submitted a confidential peer
evaluation, which is not reproduced here.
